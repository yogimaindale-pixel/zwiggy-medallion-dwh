Welcome back student. Today we are going to examine the Medallion Data Architecture layers in detail.
The Bronze layer ingests raw JSON data directly from source files, preserving original payloads and extraction timestamps.
The Silver layer parses raw JSON strings, casts data types, validates primary keys, and cleanses null values into structured tables.
The Gold layer aggregates data into analytical business marts, computing daily restaurant revenue, customer retention, and driver delivery performance.
Let's pause and understand this with an example. Bronze is unrefined mineral ore, Silver is purified metal bars, and Gold is crafted jewelry ready for retail display.
Now let's see where this appears in the repository. Bronze is managed in src bronze_layer dot py, Silver in src silver_layer dot py, and Gold in src gold_layer dot py.
Congratulations, you now understand the 3-tier Medallion Data Architecture!
